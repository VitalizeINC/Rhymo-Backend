How it flows

From word to verse, in one breath.

Rhymo is made for the middle of writing. You don't need to learn the tool first; start from one word.

  1. 1

    Give it a word

    Type the word on your mind. No vowel marks needed.

  2. 2

    See the rhymes

    By sound or by spelling. Tap syllables to change the scope, filter consonants and see sound-alikes.

  3. 3

    Keep them

    Send any good rhyme to your word bank with one tap, into the folder you choose.

  4. 4

    Write

    Write in the notepad. Rhyme ideas come from the whole dictionary, or only from your own folders.

Start and sign in

Rhymo opens with a short three-screen intro that you can skip.

  1. Open the app and read the intro, or tap “Skip”.
  2. Sign in with Apple, Google or email.
  3. If you sign up with email, enter the 6-digit code we send you.
  4. Forgot your password? Tap “Forgot your password?” to get a reset code.

Find rhymes

The heart of Rhymo. Give it a word and see its rhymes.

  1. On the home screen, tap “Find rhymes”.
  2. Type the word. No vowel marks needed; if you add them, Rhymo keeps them.
  3. Pick a mode: sound (آوایی) builds rhymes from how the word sounds; spelling (ادبی) matches the last letters.
  4. If the word can be said in more than one way, Rhymo asks “Which pronunciation?”. Tap the right one.
  5. In the results, tap syllables to change the rhyme scope. In spelling mode, choose how many last letters must match.
  6. Tap Filter to see only rhymes with the consonants you want. Sound-alikes shows words that sound the same.
  7. Send any good rhyme to your word bank and choose its folder.
No rhymes found? Choose fewer syllables or loosen the consonant filter.

Syllables

For when you work on the meter.

  1. On the home screen, tap “Syllables”.
  2. Type the word and tap “Analyze”.
  3. See the word's syllables and sounds; for example «بهار» has two: بَ · هار.

Word bank

The place for words you don't want to lose.

  1. Words come to the bank from the rhyme results or from the notepad.
  2. Make folders and name them yourself, like “love songs” or an album name.
  3. Use search to find a word, and “Remove” to take it out.
In the notepad, put the cursor on a word to add that word to your bank.

Notepad

Where the song gets written. Everything you write is saved on its own.

  1. On the home screen, tap “Notepad”, or open one of your “Recent notes”.
  2. Write. Type “/” for the next line.
  3. Put a rhyme between two “|”. All words in a group get the same color; up to 12 groups per note.
  4. While a “|” is open, rhyme ideas show at the bottom. Tap one to use it.
  5. Choose where ideas come from: the whole dictionary or only your folders.
  6. To delete a note, remove it from the home screen. This can't be undone.
/Starts the next line.
|Opens a rhyme; the next “|”, after the word, closes it: |آغاز|
||Two “|” in a row start a new rhyme group. The “New rhyme” button at the top does the same.

Settings and account

From the settings icon on the home screen.

  1. Sign out: you leave your account; your notes stay saved.
  2. Delete account: opens the account deletion page. Your account and all your writing are erased.

Questions

Do I have to add vowel marks?
No. Rhymes are built from sound. If a word can be said in more than one way, Rhymo asks which one you mean.
What is the difference between sound and spelling mode?
Sound mode listens to the word, so «هزار» rhymes with «بهار». Spelling mode looks at the last letters, so «هزار» goes with «بازار» and «گلزار».
Where is my writing saved?
On Rhymo’s server, for your account only. Details are in the privacy policy.
The word I want is not in the dictionary.
When Rhymo doesn't know a word, it asks which new words to add. Suggested words are added to the dictionary after a check.
How do I delete my account?
In Settings › Delete account, or straight from this page.

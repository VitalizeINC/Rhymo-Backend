Privacy Policy

Vitalize LLC operates Rhymo, a lyric-writing app and website. This policy describes how we handle information when you use Rhymo.

What we collect and why

Account information. We use your email address, display name, and account identifiers to sign you in and maintain your account. For email sign-in, we store a password hash and use temporary codes for verification and recovery. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the identifier and profile details that provider shares.

Writing and app activity. We store the notes, saved words, and folders you choose to save. Rhymo receives search queries and text you submit for analysis to return results. We use technical request information to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Who processes your information

Our hosting providers process information to run Rhymo. Resend processes recipient addresses and email contents to deliver verification and reset codes. Apple and Google process sign-in when you choose their services. We require service providers acting on our behalf to protect the information they process. We may disclose information when required by law.

Retention

We keep your account and saved writing while your account is active. Verification and reset codes expire after 10 minutes. We retain other records for as long as needed to operate and secure Rhymo or meet legal obligations.

Request account deletion

Email admin@vitalize.dev with the subject “Rhymo account deletion” and the email linked to your Rhymo account. We may verify that you own the account before deleting it and its associated data. We may retain records required by law or needed for security or fraud prevention.

Questions and privacy requests

Email admin@vitalize.dev to ask about your data, request access or correction, or withdraw consent where applicable.

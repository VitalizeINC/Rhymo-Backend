Privacy

Privacy Policy.

This policy covers the Rhymo app and website, run by Vitalize LLC.

Vitalize LLC8 November 2026
On this pageWhat we collect and whyWho processes your informationRetentionRequest account deletionQuestions and privacy requests

1What we collect and why

Account information

Your email address, display name and account identifiers, used to sign you in and keep your account. If you sign in with email, we also store a password hash and use temporary codes for verification and recovery. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the identifier and the profile details that provider shares.

Writing and app activity

The notes, saved words and folders you choose to keep, plus the search queries and text you submit for analysis. We use technical request information to run, secure and troubleshoot the service.

2Who processes your information

  • Hosting providers run Rhymo.
  • Resend processes recipient addresses and email contents to deliver verification and reset codes.
  • Apple and Google process sign-in when you choose them.

Service providers must protect the information they handle. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

3Retention

  • Your account and saved writing are kept while your account is active.
  • Verification and reset codes expire after 10 minutes.
  • Other records are kept as long as needed to run and secure Rhymo or to meet legal obligations.

4Request account deletion

  1. Email admin@vitalize.dev with the subject Rhymo account deletion.
  2. Include the email address linked to your Rhymo account.
  3. We may check that you own the account before we delete it and its associated data.
  4. Some records may be kept if the law requires it or if they are needed for security or fraud prevention.

5Questions and privacy requests

Email admin@vitalize.dev to ask about your data, request access or correction, or withdraw consent where this applies.

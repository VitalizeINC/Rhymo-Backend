Delete account

Request account deletion.

You can request deletion of your Rhymo account and its associated data at any time.

Request deletion by emailIn the privacy policy
On this pageHow to askFrom the app

1How to ask

  1. Email admin@vitalize.dev with the subject Rhymo account deletion.
  2. Include the email address linked to your Rhymo account.
  3. We may check that you own the account before we delete it and its associated data.
  4. Some records may be kept if the law requires it or if they are needed for security or fraud prevention.

2From the app

In the Rhymo app: Settings › Account › Delete account. This opens the account deletion guide in your browser.
